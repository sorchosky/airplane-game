# #235 Tropical islands: beaches, palms and a small resort

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/235

## Context
Owner direction, 2026-10-04: the islands in the inland sea (#223) get tropical touches: sand,
palms, a small resort. They are the one thing to fly toward across the open water, so they reward
the players who do cross, and they give the eye scale cues from the town. BotW references, in
words: Lurelin Village and Eventide Island. Thatch, pale sand, turquoise shallows, leaning palms.

Islands come from #223's pure island function (centres and radii). Models use the landmark kit
(`src/world/models/kit.ts`), merged into the landmark geometry where possible.

## Goal
Every island has wide pale beaches and palm groves, and the largest one has a small resort that
reads as a silhouette from the town and as a place at 200 m.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Beaches.** The terrain shader's sand band widens on islands so beaches run 30 to 80 m from
      the waterline. A new `sandTropical` albedo token, paler and warmer than today's sand, used
      only within the island radii. Passes the art bible §2 value targets.
- [ ] **Palms.** A palm model: curved trunk of 4 to 6 tapered segments with a lean of 10 to 30°,
      6 to 8 drooping fronds as bent flat strips, outlined, 150 triangles at most. 3 seeded
      variants by height and lean. Scattered on island beaches and lowland, leaning toward the
      water, in clusters of 3 to 9. One `InstancedMesh` plus outline, using foliage distance bands
      and the governor's density hook. Fronds sway in the vertex shader (no per-frame JS), frozen
      under reduced motion and at `?shot=`.
- [ ] **Resort.** On the largest island: a main lodge with a steep thatched roof, 4 to 6 overwater
      bungalows on stilts along a curved boardwalk, a short jetty, a few beach umbrellas and
      loungers. Thatch and timber tokens, with one accent colour shared with the fishing town
      palette so they read as the same world. Whole resort 4,000 triangles at most.
- [ ] **Night.** Resort windows and a string of boardwalk lanterns use the same emissive vertex
      mask as the fishing town (#224), warming on at the dusk key. No lights, no extra draws.
- [ ] **Readability.** From the `lake-town` station the resort's lodge roof and the palm crowns
      read as silhouettes against the haze. Post a screenshot to prove it.
- [ ] **Budget.** At a new `island-resort` bookmark: +3 draws at most, +30k triangles at most.
      Foliage stays off the resort footprint and the boardwalk.
- [ ] Model tests: triangle budgets, palms only on island ground above the waterline, resort
      footprint inside the largest island.
