# #167 [Epic] The Loop · An authored route through the landmarks, in a world with no edge

State: open · Labels: epic, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/167

## Goal

Turn the procedural landscape into a guided experience: a home basin with a cut in its ridge, a valley loop that reveals each landmark and returns home, the golden path along it, and a world that wraps so flying straight eventually comes back too. Coordination issue, not an implementation ticket.

## Context

Owner direction, 2026-10-01: the topography is good, but players need guidance toward a path that shows the landmarks, starting with a cut through the mountains at spawn that loops back to the beginning, and ideally a world that can be circled. A true sphere was rejected: circling one in a session needs a 3 to 5 km radius, which puts the horizon about 2 km away at 500 m altitude and breaks the art bible's big-sky scale (§1), and it would rewrite terrain, flight, camera, haze and clouds, which all assume flat Y-up. The owner chose a wrapping world (terrain repeats every 24 km) plus an optional visual curvature bend after a spike. Today terrain is infinite seeded noise (`src/world/heightfield.ts`, `src/world/terrainConfig.ts`), landmarks are placed by a search 2 to 5 km from spawn (#76, `src/world/landmarks.ts`), and the golden path runs from spawn through three rings and a cloud gate to the arch (`src/world/goldenPath.ts`).

## Vision

The spawn sits in a home basin ringed by ridges. One notch, the cut, faces the plane's opening heading. Fly through it and a valley carries you past each landmark in turn, each revealed by a bend so it appears framed. The valley curves back and re-enters the basin through a second notch. Nothing forces the route. It is just the easiest, most beautiful line to fly: tall landmarks pulling from afar, terrain that funnels without walls, a river to follow.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-01)

- The loop first, then the wrapping world, with the route made period-aware at that point.
- Wrapping world over a sphere. The curvature bend ships only after the spike and an owner look.
- Free flight stays open. Ridges can be cleared and nothing blocks or turns the plane.
- Loop about 13.5 km, one lap per in-game day at the 45 m/s cruise.
- Landmarks move to authored stations on the route, replacing #76's search.
- The golden path becomes the loop, superseding the 2026-09-29 golden path decision.

## Engineering constraints

- One water plane at `waterLevel` = 32 m. A river can only follow the valley where the floor sits just above it.
- Heights are built in `src/world/terrain.worker.ts`. Route and basin modifiers are pure, worker-importable, and use a spatial grid. Chunk build time is measured before and after.
- Terrain colour comes from height and slope in the shader, so carved terrain picks up correct bands automatically.
- Foliage exclusions (`src/world/foliageExclusions.ts`) follow the valley floor and landmark footprints.
