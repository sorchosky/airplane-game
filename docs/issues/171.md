# #171 Home basin and the cut

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/171

Part of #167.

## Context

The approved route design (`docs/world-route.md`, from #170) defines a home basin around spawn, a ridge ring, a cut facing spawn's heading, and a return notch. Terrain heights come from `heightAt` in `src/world/heightfield.ts`, built in `src/world/terrain.worker.ts`.

## Goal

Shape the basin, ridge and two notches into the heightfield as a pure modifier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pure modifier blends noise heights toward the basin profile by distance from spawn: a gently sloped floor, a ridge ring with natural variation (noise kept on the ridge, not a perfect circle), and two notches at the designed bearings. Smooth (C1) everywhere.
- [ ] Unit tests: ridge crest within the designed height band all round except at the notches, notch floors at the designed heights and widths, no discontinuity across the blend zone (sampled gradient bound).
- [ ] The ridge can be cleared: crest stays below a ceiling the plane reaches easily at cruise (documented in the PR with the flight model's climb numbers).
- [ ] Worker and main thread give identical heights (existing parity pattern).
- [ ] Chunk build time measured before and after, regression under 15%.
- [ ] Landmark search and foliage keep working (stations come later).
- [ ] Art bible §10 bookmarks plus `?map` before and after. New `?shot=the-cut` bookmark looking through the cut from spawn.

## Out of scope

The valley beyond the notches (#172). Landmark moves (#173).
