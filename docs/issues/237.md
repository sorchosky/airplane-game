# #237 Off-screen pointer to the next course ring

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/237

## Context
#236 shows the next three rings and points them along the loop. When the player has turned away
or wandered over the sea or a ridge, none of them may be on screen, and the player can't tell which
way the loop is. A quiet edge-of-screen chevron toward the next ring closes that gap without a map.

## Goal
When the next ring is out of view, a single chevron at the screen edge points to it, and it fades
away as soon as the ring is back in view.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A pure function takes the camera's view-projection, the next ring's world position and the
      viewport, and returns either `null` (ring in view, inside a 10% inset) or an edge position
      and angle. Rings behind the camera point the correct way, not mirrored. Unit tests for in
      view, left, right, above, below and behind.
- [ ] One chevron in the HUD, drawn in the `courseRing` colour token from #236 with the existing
      HUD text glow, `clamp(32px, 4vh, 56px)` (add a `size` token). It sits inset by the safe area
      and never overlaps the sun and moon dial or the camera preview.
- [ ] Shows only after the ring has been out of view for 1.5 s, fades in over 400 ms and out over
      200 ms. Instant under reduced motion. Hidden while paused, during the countdown and in the
      title and attract scenes.
- [ ] Works in every control mode and in both `courseRings` settings.
- [ ] Per-frame values are read with `getState()` inside the frame loop. No React state at frame
      rate, no per-frame allocations.

## Out of scope
A minimap. Distance readouts. Pointing at landmarks.
