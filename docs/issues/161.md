# #161 Beat: Into flight from either path

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/161

Part of #151.

## Context

Today flight starts with the plane at cruise and the chase camera snapping in. In the continuous scene the plane is already flying the title loop, so flight can begin from exactly where it is. Flight code only reads `ControlInput` and never imports from `pose/`.

## Goal

From Touch, Mouse or keyboard, or from Motion lock-in, the blur clears and the camera glides from the flyby framing into the chase camera while the sim takes over from the plane's current pose.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Touch and Mouse: the chosen glyph does one last tap. Its contact ring expands to the frame size and fades (400 ms) while the other frame, header and running head fade out.
- [ ] Motion: after lock-in, the feed and its frame shrink into the existing `CameraPreview` corner position (shared-element move, 700 ms), running head fades.
- [ ] Both paths: blur ramps out over 600 ms, the camera blends from the title camera to `ChaseCamera` over 1.2 s (blend in pure camera math, unit tested). The sim initialises from the flyby's position, velocity, heading and bank at that instant. A unit test asserts the first sim step is within 0.5 m of the flyby pose.
- [ ] If the flyby pose at hand-off is outside the autopilot's safe envelope, the sim starts with `active: false` and levels off before input takes over.
- [ ] Motion path enters `wings` as today after the glide. Golden path, clock and HUD start as they do now. The day-cycle clock starts advancing from the minute the title held (see #165).
- [ ] Reduced motion: no camera blend, 200 ms crossfade into the chase framing.
- [ ] e2e: keyboard, touch and replay paths reach `flying`.
