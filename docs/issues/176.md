# #176 Wrapping world: terrain repeats on a torus

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/176

Part of #167.

## Context

Owner direction: a world that can be circled. Decided as a wrapping world, not a sphere (see #167). The heightfield is seeded 2D simplex noise sampled on the plane. Sampling on a 4D torus (`simplex-noise`'s `noise4D` over two circles) makes it repeat seamlessly with period P. P = 24 km keeps every repeat outside the 10 km view distance.

## Goal

Every noise field in terrain height, terrain colour and foliage scatter repeats with period P on x and z, with no seam.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `TERRAIN_CONFIG.worldPeriod` = 24000 m. A pure `torusNoise(x, z)` maps (x, z) to two circles and samples `noise4D`, with feature sizes preserved (radius from period and frequency).
- [ ] All height noise, colour noise in the terrain shader, and foliage scatter are periodic. Unit tests: `heightAt(x, z) == heightAt(x + P, z) == heightAt(x, z + P)` within 1e-6 at many points, and a gradient bound across the seam.
- [ ] Basin, valley, river and stations are placed inside one period and their distance queries use the minimum-image (wrapped) distance.
- [ ] Chunk build time regression under 25% (4D noise costs more, measured and noted).
- [ ] The world looks the same character (`?map` and bookmarks before and after, owner review).
