# #166 Sun and moon arc replaces the clock readout

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/166

Part of #162.

## Context

`src/ui/ClockReadout.tsx` shows `HH:MM` in a filled `surface-hud` pill, top right. Art bible §8 calls for hairlines, small geometric marks and no slabs. The owner asked for a graphic time indicator: a semicircle in the corner with the sun and moon moving along it left to right. With local-time start and an accelerated day, digits next to the phone's real clock would read as wrong, so the dial carries no digits (owner decision 2026-10-01).

## Goal

A hairline semicircle dial, top right, where a sun glyph crosses the arc by day and a moon glyph crosses it by night, positioned from the same sun and moon the sky draws.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Dial: a 1 px `line` semicircle over a short horizon rule, `effect.textGlow` for legibility, no fill, no panel. Inset by the safe area at `space.md`. Its width is a new size token, legible from 3 m on a 55" TV (target about 12 vh at 1080p, owner confirms on the TV).
- [ ] Sun glyph (circle with short rays) and moon glyph (crescent) in the hairline glyph family (the stroke token from #154 if merged, otherwise 2 px). The sun is `text-primary`, the moon `text-muted`.
- [ ] Positions come from a pure function of the in-game minutes that matches the sky's sun and moon: sunrise at the left end of the horizon rule, solar noon at the top, sunset at the right end, and the same for the moon by night. Unit tested against the sun and moon direction functions the sky already uses, so the dial and the sky can't disagree.
- [ ] At sunrise and sunset the outgoing glyph fades out below the horizon rule as the incoming one fades in. No glyph is ever off the arc.
- [ ] No digits anywhere player-facing, including the pause menu. `aria-label` keeps the time (`copy.hud.clockLabel` plus `HH:MM`) for screen readers. The numeric time stays available in the `?debug` overlay (added by #165).
- [ ] Still hidden in `?shot=` captures and during calibrate. Re-renders follow the same budget as today (React subscribes to a coarse value, glyph motion is driven from a store read in an animation frame or with CSS transforms, no React state at frame rate).
- [ ] High-contrast mode adds a `surface-hud` disc behind the dial (existing high-contrast pattern).
- [ ] Screenshots at `?time=` 06:00, 09:00, 12:00, 18:30, 21:00, 00:00.
