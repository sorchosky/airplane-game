# #129 QA1 · Remove ambient birds and descent wind streaks

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/129

## Context

Batch 1, visual clutter and HUD clarity. Follow-up to #77 and #81. The owner wants the black dash-like shapes in the sky and the vertical lines seen during descent removed, not redesigned.

Current main has ambient bird formations and camera-facing wind ribbons in `src/flight/FlightVfx.tsx`. `src/world/GoldenPath.tsx` also renders a separate bird flock. Removing only the ambient formation would leave the same visual language in the authored route. The diagonal trails attached to the wingtips are a separate effect.

## Goal

A clean sky and unobstructed descent, without removing useful flight, contact, or route feedback.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Remove bird rendering from both FlightVfx and GoldenPath, including their geometry/material allocation, instance updates, and disposal paths. Confirm no black dash formations remain in free flight or on the golden path.
- [ ] Remove the camera-facing wind streak/ribbon effect at every speed and quality tier. Do not merely rotate the vertical lines or hide them under reduced motion.
- [ ] Preserve wingtip vortices, cloud effects, dust/spray, camera FOV/shake, flight audio, route rings, cloud gate, and arch completion. Do not delete shared speed-intensity math still used by retained effects.
- [ ] Remove exclusively unused bird/streak constants and resources. Keep shared tokens/helpers if another live feature uses them.
- [ ] Update affected tests to verify retained behavior, especially low-pass dust/spray and route completion after bird refs are removed. No early-return path may accidentally disable retained effects.
- [ ] Record the owner's explicit removal as a new decision superseding only the bird/streak portions of the #77 VFX decision and relevant art-bible §7 requirements. Keep unrelated effects intact.
- [ ] Report before/after draw calls and frame-time measurements for an identical route. The removed effects must produce no draw calls or per-frame instance work.

## Out of scope

Wingtip trail redesign, all other VFX tuning, audio changes, flight model changes, and replacement wildlife.

## Depends on

None. Implementations associated with #77 and #81 are present on inspected main. This is a targeted removal, not a reimplementation of those tickets.

## Likely files

`src/flight/FlightVfx.tsx`, `src/flight/flightVfxMath.ts` and tests, `src/world/GoldenPath.tsx`, `src/styles/tokens.ts` only for unused tokens, golden-path e2e tests, `docs/art-bible.md` §7, a new decision file.

## Verification

- Fly level, dive at full input, boost, bank, perform low passes over water and land, and fly through the golden path.
- Repeat at low/medium/high quality and with reduced motion on/off.
- Capture the mountain view and a five-second descent frame strip. Verify no vertical screen streaks or birds, and that diagonal wingtip trails remain.
- `npm run check` and affected e2e specs pass locally and in CI.
