# #135 QA7 · Replace sparse grass cards with a continuous painted ground surface

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/135

## Context

Batch 3, terrain and ground surfaces. Follow-up to #75 and QA6 (#134). The owner finds the sparse grass texture disconnected from the scene and is leaning toward removing it, either leaving green terrain or using an integrated ground texture.

Current main renders a separate instanced `Grass` layer of alpha-masked cards, with density and altitude fade, sway, and downwash. The ground already has shader-based color variation. Recommended direction for this ticket is to remove the grass cards and retain subtle painted ground variation, rather than increasing card density or introducing downloaded grass photography.

### QA photo references

- `IMG_0272.jpeg`, primary reference, scattered triangular grass clumps look stamped onto the hillside, with visible gaps and a different texture scale from the terrain.
- `IMG_0271.jpeg`, foreground clumps and distant specks do not read as continuous ground cover.
- `IMG_0268.jpeg`, small dark grass marks add noise to the night foreground.

Photo filenames refer to the original screenshots supplied with the owner's September 28 QA, reviewed September 29. They are reference identifiers, not repository paths or embedded GitHub attachments.

## Goal

A continuous, calm ground surface that belongs to the terrain, with readable slopes and enough broad variation to support low flight.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Remove the separate grass-card layer from normal flight at every quality tier. No stray cards or altitude/density transitions remain.
- [ ] Remove grass-only geometry, masks, buffers, scatter/cache updates, sway/downwash uniforms, and cleanup paths where no longer used. Do not leave an invisible component doing CPU/GPU work.
- [ ] Preserve trees, bushes, boulders, their shared density controls, exclusion zones, and deterministic scatter. Removing Grass must not stop shared foliage uniforms from being updated.
- [ ] Use the existing terrain grass bands and QA6's procedural surface treatment for a coherent green base with restrained broad variation. Retain sun-facing/cool-shadow response and natural slope transitions.
- [ ] In the PR provide a same-camera comparison of the selected subtle procedural treatment and a flat-color baseline with surface variation disabled for the comparison only. Ship the subtle procedural treatment by default, without adding a player setting or a second production rendering path.
- [ ] No small stamped blade motifs, checkerboard repetition, shimmering high-frequency ground noise, world-space seams, or sudden appearance changes with altitude.
- [ ] Keep low-flight speed/height cues from terrain relief, trees, rocks, water edges, contact shadow, and retained VFX. Do not compensate by adding new particles or restoring the removed wind streaks.
- [ ] Update affected grass-specific tests and counters while retaining shared foliage regression tests. Report the saved draw calls/triangles and confirm there is no grass-only per-frame work.
- [ ] Document the owner's QA direction in a new decision file superseding only the grass-card/downwash requirements from #75 and art-bible §7. The shared procedural terrain and foliage decisions remain in force.
