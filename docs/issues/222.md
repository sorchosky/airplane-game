# #222 Mountains along the loop, and a broken basin rim

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/222

## Context
The loop runs through rolling green hills. Real mountains only show in the far north
(`docs/screenshots/176-mountain-vista-after.png`). The home basin rim is a near-perfect circle on
the map. The art bible's Tabantha reference (§1) is the target: tall stratified rock below a huge
sky, stacked in aerial perspective.

## Concept art
Owner-reviewed sketch, 2026-10-04. Hatched areas are the massifs: north and south of the inland
sea (#223), plus one inside the ring. Positions are a starting point. The acceptance criteria win.

## Goal
The loop is flanked by real mountain massifs, and the basin rim reads as a mountain ring with
peaks and saddles, not a crater.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Massifs.** A mountain mask raises ridged-noise ranges outside the valley's flank fade. Peaks
      reach 450 to 900 m (versus the 260 m basin crest today), with at least 3 summits above
      `snowHeight` visible from the loop. The mask fades to zero at the valley's outer flank so
      the roughened valley walls (#221) stay authored.
- [ ] **Inside the ring.** The land between the basin rim and the valley rises into at least one
      massif, so flying the loop has mountains on both sides for at least 40% of its length.
- [ ] **Keep the sea clear.** The mask is zero inside the inland sea's footprint (#223, about
      x 5450 to 10450, z -2400 to 5600, plus 300 m). East of the loop, place massifs north and
      south of that footprint so they frame the coast on either side of the town site, not in
      front of it.
- [ ] **Basin rim.** The crest varies ±35% round the ring (from 12%), with 3 to 5 named saddles
      and 2 or 3 peaks over 400 m. The two notches keep their floors and widths exactly.
- [ ] **Sightlines.** Every landmark reveal and station framing in `docs/world-route.md` still
      holds. Gaps in the new ranges stay along the listed sightlines. The tests pass.
- [ ] **Title flyby room.** The 700 m clear radius and the title flyby loop are unchanged.
      `?shot=title` still has the masthead's left third over sky.
- [ ] Toroidal wrap still seamless (`wrap.test.ts`, `torusNoise.test.ts`). Mountain noise samples
      through `torusNoise`.
- [ ] Chunk build time within +10% of `main`. Triangle count at `mountain-vista` and `spawn` within
      +15% (LOD rings unchanged).
