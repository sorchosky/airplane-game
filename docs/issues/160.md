# #160 Beat: Choose to Position (Motion frame becomes the calibration frame)

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/160

Part of #151.

## Context

Choosing Motion moves through `permission` to `calibrate`. Today `CalibrateScreen` replaces everything with the camera feed (4:3 at full screen height, 12 px frame, per the 2026-09-26 calibration decision), a target T-pose and the skeleton overlay. In the continuous scene the Motion choice itself becomes calibration: its corner marks open out into the calibration frame and its hairline figure grows into the target. The world stays blurred in the side bands the 4:3 feed leaves on wider screens.

## Goal

Choosing Motion transforms the Motion frame into the calibration frame in place, with the camera ask and feed arriving inside it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On choosing Motion: the Touch frame and header fade (300 ms). The Motion frame's four corner marks travel to the corners of the 4:3 full-height calibration frame (shared-element move, 700 ms ease out) and the label fades.
- [ ] The Motion glyph scales and moves to become the target T-pose at the calibration's target size and position (0.135 frame-width shoulder span), then crossfades into the existing target silhouette over 300 ms. The tilt loop eases to 0° before the move.
- [ ] Camera ask copy appears under the frame while the browser prompt is up: one line from `copy`, "Prop your phone up, then step back" (with the existing privacy line kept if it fits on one more line). On deny, the existing error path still works.
- [ ] Once the feed is live it fades in inside the frame over 400 ms. The running head stays top left. The blurred world stays visible in the side bands.
- [ ] Calibration checks, copy table, skeleton overlay, hold ring and lock-in logic are unchanged (only their container and entrance change). Existing calibrate tests pass.
- [ ] Quit from calibrate reverses to Choose.
- [ ] Reduced motion: crossfade, no shared-element moves.
