# #5 [Epic] M4 Polish

State: open · Labels: epic, milestone:M4 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/5

## Goal
Smooth on a real phone while casting, and pleasant to play from the couch. Covers a performance pass, the pause menu, orientation handling, reduced motion, UX copy and (optionally) audio.

## Done when
- All sub-issues are closed.
- A steady 30+ fps render on the owner's phone while mirroring to the TV, with pose detection at 15+ Hz.
- No screen after Start needs a touch.

## Owner checkpoint
A full play session of about 10 minutes on the TV setup. Note heat, frame drops and anything confusing.
