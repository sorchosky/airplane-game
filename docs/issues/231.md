# #231 Rock outcrops and boulders on shoulders and ridges

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/231

## Context
Steep terrain is a smooth beige ramp (`176-valley-tower-approach-after.png`). BotW breaks every
slope with stratified grey-beige rock, lighter on top (art bible §1). Instanced geometry is the
cheapest way to get that silhouette on a phone, cheaper than a triplanar cliff shader. The large
waterfall cliff also clips to near white in sun (`174-river-lake-before-after.png`).

## Goal
Slopes and ridges carry rock outcrops and boulders that break the smooth ramps, at a small fixed
draw cost.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] 4 base meshes from the landmark kit: 2 slab outcrops with horizontal strata steps, 2 rounded
      boulders, 60 to 150 triangles each, seeded so they are identical every load.
- [ ] Scattered on slopes 35 to 60° and along ridge crests, denser on the cliff side of the valley
      walls, using the existing scatter cells and wrapped seeds. Never inside the valley floor,
      landmark footprints or the town pad (#223, if merged).
- [ ] One `InstancedMesh` per base mesh with outlines. Distance bands like foliage, so the far edge
      thins first and the governor's foliage density hook applies with no popping.
- [ ] Grey-beige albedo tokens, lighter top faces via vertex colour. Rock albedo value is capped so
      sunlit faces stay under the art bible §2 value ceiling, and the waterfall cliff uses it too.
- [ ] Budget at `valley-south-shoulder`: +4 draws at most, +40k triangles at most.
- [ ] Unit tests for the slope and exclusion rules.

## Out of scope
Terrain shader changes. Snow on rocks.
