# #178 Spike: horizon curvature bend

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/178

Part of #167.

## Context

The owner floated a curved world and approved a quick spike before deciding. Flight stays flat (Y up), but a vertex-shader drop toward the horizon, `y -= d² / 2R` with d the horizontal distance from the camera, can suggest a round world. It has to work with the haze (which blends to the sky colour by 9 km) and must not make landmarks or clouds float.

## Goal

A flag-gated prototype and a contact sheet so the owner can decide whether to ship it and at what radius.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `?curve=<km>` applies the drop in the shared vertex path used by terrain, water, foliage, landmarks and clouds (one include), off by default.
- [ ] Contact sheet: `spawn`, `mountain-vista`, `toward-sun` and `the-cut` at R = off, 80, 50, 30 km, at 100 m and 800 m altitude.
- [ ] Notes on artefacts (haze line, cloud layer, contact shadow) and frame-time cost.
- [ ] Owner decides on the PR. If approved, a follow-up ticket ships it. If not, the flag is removed before merge.

## Out of scope

Shipping it on by default.
