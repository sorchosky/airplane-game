# #233 Water: sun sparkle and shallow shelves

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/233

## Context
The water reads as flat cyan with a uniform sand band. BotW water has hard white sun glints and a
clear shallow-to-deep step. The water shader is `src/world/waterShader.ts`, and the terrain already
tints the lake bed by depth.

This matters more once the 5 × 8 km inland sea (#223) lands. That much flat water looks dead
without sparkle, and the tropical islands (#235) rely on turquoise shallows to read as tropical.

## Goal
Water sparkles toward the sun and shows a readable shallow shelf, with no extra render passes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Two-band stepped depth tint: shallows (first 3 m) a lighter turquoise, then deep, using the
      existing bed-height data. New tokens if colours are added.
- [ ] Toon sun glints: thresholded scrolling noise times the sun reflection term, drawn as small
      hard-edged near-white flecks, strongest toward the sun. At night only a thin moon path.
- [ ] Glint noise uses at least two scales so no tiling repeat is visible across a wide open-water
      view (the `sea-horizon` bookmark if #223 has merged, otherwise `mountain-vista`).
- [ ] Shore foam keeps its line but breaks up with noise.
- [ ] No reflections, no extra passes, no new draws. Glints freeze under reduced motion and at
      `?shot=`.
- [ ] Works on all three post tiers. On `low` the glints still read without bloom.

## Out of scope
Waves, wakes, refraction.

## Depends on
none

## Likely files
`src/world/waterShader.ts`, `src/world/Water.tsx`, `src/world/terrainMaterial.ts`,
`src/styles/tokens.ts`.

## Verification
`npm run check`. Bookmarks `river-lake`, `lake-bank`, `toward-sun` at morning, golden hour and
night, plus `sea-horizon` and `island-resort` if they exist by then.

## Suggested model
Claude Sonnet 5.5. Codex: default model at medium reasoning effort. Shader-only and self-contained.
