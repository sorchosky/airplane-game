# #232 Forest masses: clumped groves and far canopy impostors

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/232

## Context
Trees are an even random sprinkle (`docs/screenshots/176-valley-tower-approach-after.png`). BotW
alternates dense forest blocks with open meadow, and that contrast is what reads at speed.
Clumping also lets the far field swap individual trees for cheap canopy masses. Foliage is
scattered per #75 with a grove noise (`groveScale` in `terrainConfig.ts`).

## Goal
Forests read as masses with open meadows between, and far forest costs fewer triangles than today.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] At least 70% of trees fall inside groves, and meadows between groves are near empty (under 5%
      of today's density).
- [ ] Beyond 1.5 km, each grove draws as 1 to 3 merged low-poly canopy blobs (lumpy hulls, dark
      green with a lit cap, outlined) instead of its trees. The handoff between trees and blobs
      fades with the per-instance `keep` so nothing pops.
- [ ] Conifers dominate slopes above the existing conifer line.
- [ ] Spawn foliage and the basin rules from #171 still hold.
- [ ] Net triangles at `mountain-vista` ≤ `main`. Draws +2 at most.
- [ ] Unit tests for the grove share and the far-blob switch distance.

## Out of scope
Grass cards. New tree models. Rocks (#231).
