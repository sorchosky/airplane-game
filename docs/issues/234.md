# #234 Cumulus scale pass: mid-ground towers for parallax

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/234

## Context
In flight the sky is mostly empty with small clouds far off
(`docs/screenshots/176-mountain-vista-after.png`). Big cumulus at 1 to 3 km is how BotW sells
altitude and speed, and parallax against them makes the 45 m/s cruise feel fast. Clouds are toon
impostors from #70 (`src/world/Clouds.tsx`).

The inland sea (#223) adds a 5 × 8 km open-water view east of the town, where clouds are the main
depth cue.

## Goal
2 or 3 tall cumulus towers are in view from anywhere on the loop, at no extra draw cost.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] 6 to 10 tall cumulus, 2 to 3× today's largest, placed near the loop at 300 to 600 m base
      altitude, so 2 or 3 are in the chase view at any route `s`.
- [ ] At least 3 of them sit out over the inland sea footprint (about x 5450 to 10450,
      z -2400 to 5600) at 2 to 6 km from the town site, stepping back into the haze for depth.
      This holds whether or not #223 has merged, since placement is by coordinates.
- [ ] Cream tops, lavender-grey undersides, stepped ramp per art bible §1, from the existing cloud
      material and lighting presets.
- [ ] None blocks a landmark sightline in `docs/world-route.md`. The cloud gate from #163 is
      unchanged.
- [ ] Draws +0. Placement wraps with the world.
- [ ] Unit test for the in-view count along the route.

## Out of scope
Volumetric clouds. Weather.

## Depends on
none

## Likely files
`src/world/Clouds.tsx`, `src/world/cloudMath.ts`, `src/world/cloudStore.ts`, tests.

## Verification
`npm run check`. Bookmarks `clouds`, `mountain-vista`, `valley-east-river` before and after, plus
`sea-horizon` if it exists by then.
