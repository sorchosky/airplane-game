# #58 [Epic] M5 AAA polish pass

State: open · Labels: epic, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/58

## Goal
Take Driftwing from a competent prototype to a portfolio showstopper: a stranger picks it up with no explanation, is flying and grinning inside 60 seconds, and asks what engine it is built in. Three pillars: art direction (Breath of the Wild), engineering (feel and frame time), experience (welcoming first run).

Brief and audit: `docs/art-bible.md`, `docs/perf.md`, `docs/experience.md`, `docs/aaa-backlog.md` (audit PR).

## Done when
- All slices above are closed.
- `docs/showcase.md` exists with five hero shots, the measured perf and latency table, and the case study.
- 60 fps sustained on the owner's phone on `medium` while casting; gesture-to-visible-bank under 120 ms (excluding mirroring); zero per-frame allocations in the hot path.
- Title to flight under 60 s for a first-timer on the recorded fixture.
