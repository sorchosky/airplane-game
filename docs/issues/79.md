# #79 A9 · UI reskin: thin-line chrome, glow instead of panels, blurred pause

State: open · Labels: size:M, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/79

Part of epic #58 (M5 AAA polish pass). Slice A9 in `docs/aaa-backlog.md`. Art bible section 8.

## Context
UI chrome is generic rounded rectangles on an 80 % dark panel. Paused and countdown cover the world with an opaque-looking slab. The camera preview can show as a flat cream box. The Driftwing title has the right language; nothing else follows it.

## Goal
Carry the title's type and chrome language into every other screen.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] New UI tokens from the art bible (`line`, `glow`, translucent `surface-hud` with backdrop blur, cool `text-muted` and `control-inactive`); `tokens.css` and the contrast test kept in sync.
- [ ] Prompts: text with the `glow` text-shadow and a hairline rule, no panel for single lines.
- [ ] Pause and countdown: the world blurred (`backdrop-filter`) and desaturated under a 60 % cool scrim; menu items as hairline frames with the hold ring; the countdown numerals in the wordmark treatment.
- [ ] Camera preview: hairline frame in `accent` / `control-inactive`, transparent background (no cream box), corner radius `sharp`.
- [ ] Calibrate, error and orientation screens on the same language.
- [ ] Every text element WCAG AA against its worst-case backdrop, asserted in `tokens.test.ts`.
- [ ] Reduced motion: no blur transitions, fades only.
- [ ] Screenshots of every screen in the PR, before and after.
