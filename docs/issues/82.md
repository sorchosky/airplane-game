# #82 X5 · Accessibility: seated mode, handedness, high-contrast HUD, captions, AA audit

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/82

Part of epic #58 (M5 AAA polish pass). Slice X5 in `docs/aaa-backlog.md`. Accessibility table in `docs/experience.md`.

## Goal
Everyone who can raise their arms can play, and everyone can read the screen.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Seated mode (`?seated` and a pause-menu toggle, persisted): arms-only gate (no hip check in calibration), 1.5 × deadzones, pitch from wrist height alone. Pure params variants with tests.
- [ ] Handedness: calibration accepts a ±15° neutral roll; the gate tolerates asymmetric arm height.
- [ ] High-contrast HUD option: readout and prompts on a full-alpha panel with thicker lines.
- [ ] Captions: every audio cue (F3's `caption` events) shows a 1 s caption in `tv-caption` at bottom centre; toggle in the pause menu, persisted.
- [ ] Reduced motion audited across every slice landed so far: title, camera, VFX, transitions.
- [ ] WCAG AA on every text element, including the title screen, asserted in `tokens.test.ts` against each element's worst-case backdrop.
- [ ] Strings in `src/ui/copy.ts`.
