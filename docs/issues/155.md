# #155 Orientation prompt: frameless rotate cue and safe-area fix

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/155

Part of #151.

## Context

`src/ui/OrientationPrompt.tsx` shows a bordered `surface-hud` card (icon, "Turn your phone sideways", "Driftwing flies in landscape.") over `surface-scrim`. On iPhone the status bar area shows a bright blue strip, the scrim's desaturation turns the warm backdrop muddy brown, and the heading wraps unevenly. iOS Safari can't lock orientation, so this is the first thing many iPhone players see.

## Goal

A frameless prompt where motion carries the instruction, covering the full screen including safe areas.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No card. Icon and `copy.orientation.title` sit directly on the scrim with `effect.textGlow`. The body line is removed from `copy.orientation`.
- [ ] Heading uses `text-wrap: balance` and fits on at most two lines at 390 px width.
- [ ] Icon redrawn with the glyph stroke token from #154 if merged, otherwise 2 px. A phone outline rotates 90° clockwise into landscape over 1.2 s, holds 1 s, loops. Reduced motion shows the landscape end state.
- [ ] The scrim covers the full viewport including all safe-area insets (no strip under the iOS status bar), and the page background behind it matches.
- [ ] No desaturation on this scrim (cool tint plus blur only).
- [ ] Pause-on-portrait behaviour unchanged (existing tests pass).
