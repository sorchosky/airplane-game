# #154 Control choice: viewfinder frames and animated hairline glyphs

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/154

Part of #151.

## Context

`ControlSelectScreen` shows two option cards. On hover or focus a card fills with `surface-hud` and glows, with a 2 px `accent` border plus a 4 px outline. iOS keeps `:hover` after a tap, so the last-tapped card looks selected. The owner's mockup replaces this: header "CHOOSE YOUR CONTROL SETUP" in tracked uppercase, two corner-mark frames with no fill, a hairline T-pose figure labelled MOTION and a touch hand labelled TOUCH. Owner asked for subtle loops: the torso tilts left and right, and the touch hand does a soft tap and drag. This ticket builds the component. The stage and transitions come later.

## Goal

A `ControlChoice` component matching the mockup, with one consistent glyph family and the two loops, used by the existing control select screen.

## Acceptance criteria

Layout and type

- [ ] Header from `copy.controlSelect.title`, set to "How will you fly" (`fontDisplay`, `tv-title`, uppercase, `trackingDisplay`), centred.
- [ ] Two equal frames with no border and no fill, only four corner marks (24 px arms, 2 px, `line`). Centred glyph box of identical size in each, label below. Spacing via `space.*` on the 8pt grid. Minimum target 64 px.
- [ ] Labels "Motion" and "Touch" (or "Mouse" on non-touch devices) in `fontBody`, `weightStart`, uppercase, 0.3em tracking, at least `tv-body`. Glyphs bottom-aligned in their boxes so the labels share a baseline. Hint strings removed from `copy.controlSelect`.

Glyph family

- [ ] One SVG family: a single stroke-width token (new, 2 px at 1080p, `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`), round caps and joins, `currentColor`, no fills.
- [ ] Motion: head, neck, shoulder contour, arms straight out ending in a rounded taper (no fingers), torso sides open at the waist, as in the mockup.
- [ ] Touch: an index-finger hand drawn in the same contour style (not the stock icon), plus a contact dot. Mouse: a cursor arrow in the same style.

Motion (all loops built from a pure keyframe module with unit tests)

- [ ] Motion loop, 3.6 s: torso, head and arms rotate together about the waist point, 0° → −10° → 0° → +10° → 0°, sine ease in-out, 400 ms hold at each extreme. Head counter-tilts 3°.
- [ ] Touch loop, 3.2 s: hand eases down 6 px and scales to 0.97 (160 ms), a contact ring expands from the fingertip 0 → 28 px radius while fading out (500 ms), hand and dot drag 40 px along a shallow arc (900 ms, ease in-out) leaving a 1 px trail that fades over 400 ms, hand lifts 6 px and the dot fades, 600 ms rest. Drag direction alternates each loop.
- [ ] Mouse loop: the same tap and drag beats with the cursor and a click ring.
- [ ] Both glyphs loop continuously, phase-offset so they never move in sync. Transforms and opacity only.

States

- [ ] Focus or selection turns the corner marks `accent` and slides each 8 px inward over 200 ms. No fill, no glow, no outline ring. The focus indicator holds ≥3:1 against the backdrop (assert in `tokens.test.ts`).
- [ ] Hover styles only under `@media (hover: hover)`.
- [ ] Motion is focused by default. Arrow keys move focus, Enter or Space chooses.
- [ ] Reduced motion: static key pose for each glyph (Motion at 0°, Touch mid-drag), corner marks change colour without sliding.
- [ ] Exposes refs for the frame corners and the Motion glyph so later beat tickets can animate them as shared elements.
