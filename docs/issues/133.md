# #133 QA5 · Remove tree and rock halos and integrate foliage with the landscape

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/133

## Context

Batch 2, silhouette integration. Follow-up to #75. Trees, bushes, and rocks read like stickers, with a pale band inside a heavy dark border. The owner wants them to feel incorporated into the environment, using Breath of the Wild as the visual reference.

Current `foliageMaterial.ts` applies rim lighting to foliage bodies and renders a separate inverted hull. Both use growth/fade paths. The screenshot alone cannot distinguish a bright rim from an actual geometry gap. Diagnose both before changing thickness.

### QA photo references

- `IMG_0272.jpeg`, primary reference. Large tree left of the aircraft and trees on the right have broad cream/white edges, while nearby boulders show the same cutout treatment.
- `IMG_0271.jpeg`, trees on the left slope and foreground rocks show detached-looking edging.
- `IMG_0268.jpeg`, night foliage still reads as individually outlined stickers.

Photo filenames refer to the original screenshots supplied with the owner's September 28 QA, reviewed September 29. They are reference identifiers, not repository paths or embedded GitHub attachments.

## Goal

Foliage that sits within the same light and atmosphere as the ground, with attached, restrained silhouettes and no white padding.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Diagnose body rim lighting versus hull separation using a body-only, hull-only, and rim-disabled comparison. Document which paths cause the reported halo.
- [ ] Remove the broad all-around pale rim. Keep only subtle directional edge lighting if it reads as illumination, not white padding. Scope any rim-strength control to foliage rather than globally changing the plane or clouds.
- [ ] Hulls follow the actual canopy, trunk, bush, and rock silhouette with no visible background gap. Body/hull transforms, instance scales, growth, and fade remain aligned for nonuniformly scaled instances.
- [ ] Use thinner, lower-emphasis near-black linework than the plane, with at most the current 2 CSS px cap. Distant foliage blends through the shared haze rather than becoming dark cutouts.
- [ ] Retain readable low-poly light/shadow masses and subdued, ground-compatible greens and rock tones. Preserve the approved palette. The BotW direction is softer material integration, not adding fine photographic detail.
- [ ] Confirm trunks and rocks meet the sampled ground without floating or visible gaps during streaming and quality-density transitions. Make only localized placement fixes if needed, with pure regression tests.
- [ ] Compare low/medium/high quality, DPR 0.75 and 1.5, close and mid-distance views, and daylight/dusk/night. No outline popping, shimmering, detached hulls during growth, or loss of tree/rock identity.
- [ ] Remain within the existing foliage budget of fewer than 12 draws and 120k triangles on medium. No per-object shadow maps, new per-tree materials, or new global render pass.
- [ ] Include before/after crops for a round tree, conifer, bush, and boulder, plus a full landscape frame. Record any lasting foliage outline/rim rationale in a new decision file.

## Out of scope

Tree-model replacement, higher tree density, grass removal, terrain texture changes, plane outline changes, and a new shadow system.
