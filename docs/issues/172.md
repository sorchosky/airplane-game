# #172 Carve the route valley

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/172

Part of #167.

## Context

The basin and notches exist (#171). The loop (`src/world/route.ts`, `src/world/routePoints.ts`) now needs to become a valley that funnels the eye and the plane without walls.

## Goal

Carve a valley along the route between the two notches: a floor at the route's designed heights and flanks that rise into the existing terrain.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pure modifier using `route.nearest`: inside half the floor width, height eases to the floor height. Across the flank zone, height blends back to noise, with flanks lifted so they read as valley walls (lift amount in `terrainConfig`). C1 smooth.
- [ ] Noise detail kept on the floor at reduced amplitude so it doesn't look graded flat.
- [ ] Unit tests: floor clearance along the whole route, flanks above the floor by the designed margin on both sides, continuity at notch joins with the basin.
- [ ] Worker parity, chunk build time regression under 15% (spatial grid from the route module).
- [ ] Bookmarks plus `?map` before and after, and a 30 s recording following the valley.

## Out of scope

Landmarks (#173), river (#174), golden path (#175).
