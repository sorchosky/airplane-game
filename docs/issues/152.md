# #152 Front door direction: decision records and doc updates

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/152

Part of #151.

## Context

The owner approved a new front door on 2026-10-01 (see the epic). Several records contradict it: the 2026-09-26 `ui` entries in `docs/decisions.md` for the band sweep, `TitleSky`, the centred wordmark and the Start snapshot hand-off, the title-only rule for the uppercase tracked display treatment in `docs/art-direction.md`, frames 00 and 01 in `docs/experience.md`, and the hands-free rule in `CLAUDE.md`. Implementation tickets must cite these decisions rather than re-litigate them, so they land first.

## Goal

Record the front door direction as decision files and update the docs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] New decision files under `docs/decisions/`, one log-format line each (`date · area · decision · why`), named `2026-10-01-<issue#>-<slug>.md`:
  - [ ] `ui` The front door is one continuous scene over the live world. A persistent stage maps game states to beats (Poster, Masthead, Choose, Position, Flight) and transitions elements in place instead of mounting separate screens. Owner request for a flow that progresses with each choice.
  - [ ] `ui` The title is a live flyby at the active lighting preset. `TitleSky` remains only as the boot poster and failure fallback. Supersedes the 2026-09-26 `TitleSky` background entry.
  - [ ] `ui` Asymmetric masthead: wordmark, hairline rule and Start in the left third over sky, inset by the safe area, plane in the right two thirds, text never on the plane's screen path. Supersedes the centred wordmark layout.
  - [ ] `ui` The frosted band sweep and the wordmark's offset shadow layer retire. The wordmark reveals with a tracking-in fade. Supersedes the 2026-09-26 band and #73 sweep entries.
  - [ ] `ui` Tagline and pose silhouette leave the title. Body-control teaching lives in the Motion option's animated glyph. Supersedes `docs/experience.md` frame 00's tagline and silhouette.
  - [ ] `ui` Control choice: corner-mark viewfinder frames with no fill, one hairline glyph family, labels in Start's treatment (Work Sans 600, uppercase, 0.3em). The camera mode is labelled "Motion" to players. Focus turns the corner marks `accent` and slides them 8 px inward.
  - [ ] `art` The uppercase tracked display treatment extends from the wordmark to front-door section headers at `tv-title`. Narrowly supersedes the title-only rule in `docs/art-direction.md`.
  - [ ] `render` Front-door blur is rendered in the pipeline (downsampled blur pass) on `medium` and `high`. On `low` the world freezes to a still blurred once. No `backdrop-filter` over the live canvas.
  - [ ] `ui` Front-door motion uses the Web Animations API. No animation library.
- [ ] `CLAUDE.md` Rules: "Hands-free after Start" becomes "Hands-free after the control choice", with one sentence saying the control choice is the last touch.
- [ ] `docs/experience.md` frames 00 to 03 rewritten as the beats table from the epic.
- [ ] `docs/art-direction.md` "Main title treatment" bullet notes the section-header exception and points at the new decision file.
- [ ] `docs/decisions.md` is not edited.
