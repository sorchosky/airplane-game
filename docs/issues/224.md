# #224 Fishing town landmark: houses, dock, boathouse, lighthouse

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/224

## Context
The landmark kit (`src/world/models/kit.ts`) builds low-poly models that merge into one toon draw
and one outline draw. The town is the first landmark that is a cluster, so it has to read at
1.5 km (reveal) as a silhouette and at 200 m as a place. It sits on the west shore of the 5 × 8 km
inland sea (#223), so it is a harbour town looking out over open water. BotW references, in words:
Lurelin and Hateno. Whitewashed walls, timber framing, steep roofs, a pier on stilts.

## Goal
A small fishing town on the shore pad from #223. Clear silhouette from the reveal, charming detail
at the station.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Silhouette.** One tall element, a lighthouse or a windmill, at least 3× the house ridge
      height, on the point nearest the route. It is what reads at the reveal.
- [ ] **Houses.** 8 to 12 houses from 3 parametric variants (one-storey cottage, two-storey,
      boathouse with a water door). Gable roofs at 40 to 50°, deep eaves, a chimney on half. Seeded
      jitter in size, rotation (±8° off the street line) and roof colour. Arranged on 1 or 2 curved
      lanes following the shore contour, never a grid.
- [ ] **Dock.** A main pier 60 to 90 m long on pilings, one or two finger piers, a few crates and
      barrels, a net-drying rack on shore. Pilings sit on the sea bed via `LocalGround`.
- [ ] **Palette.** New albedo tokens in `src/styles/tokens.ts`: wall lime-white, timber umber, two
      roof colours (terracotta and slate teal), pier weathered grey. Each passes the art bible §2
      value targets and keeps outline ≥3:1 against water and grass. #235 reuses one accent.
- [ ] **Night.** Windows carry an emissive vertex mask that warms on at the dusk key and off at
      morning, driven by the existing day-cycle uniforms. No lights, no extra draws. Build it so
      #235 can reuse it.
- [ ] **Budget.** Whole town ≤ 6,000 triangles, merged into the existing landmark geometry. Draw
      calls at `lake-town` +0 if merged, +2 at most if it must be separate.
- [ ] Foliage stays out of the footprint. The town sits flat on the pad with no floating corners.

## Out of scope

puffs). People and animals.
