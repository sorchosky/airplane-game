# #27 Mobile performance pass and debug HUD

State: open · Labels: milestone:M4, size:M, agent-ready, needs-human · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/27

## Context
The target phone does four heavy things at once: camera capture, MediaPipe inference, 3D rendering, and encoding the screen mirror to the TV. Heat and throttling are real risks during a 10-minute session. Targets from epic #5 are 30+ fps rendering and 15+ Hz pose detection while casting.

## Goal
Measure, then make the game hold its targets on the owner's phone while casting.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `?debug` perf HUD: render fps (1 s avg and 1% low), frame ms, pose Hz, inference ms, draw calls, triangles, DPR, quality tier. Small, top-right, TV-legible.
- [ ] Adaptive quality: a rolling frame-time monitor steps DPR (for example 1.5 → 1.25 → 1.0 → 0.75) and then the post-FX tier (#26) down when over budget for 3 s, and back up cautiously. Hysteresis prevents oscillation.
- [ ] Pose detection rate adapts too (20 → 15 → 12 Hz) if inference time is high, and never drops below 12
- [ ] Cap DPR at 1.5 on mobile by default. The TV mirror resolution doesn't benefit from more.
- [ ] An audit and fix list: shared geometries and materials, no per-frame allocations in `useFrame` hot paths (check with a quick allocation profile), frustum culling on, chunk builds spread across frames (time-sliced) if they cause hitches
- [ ] Evaluate moving MediaPipe into a Web Worker (with OffscreenCanvas / ImageBitmap transfer). Implement it only if main-thread measurements show inference blocking frames and iOS Safari supports the path. Otherwise document the finding in `docs/decisions.md`.
- [ ] **Owner step (needs-human):** play 10 min on the phone while casting with `?debug` and paste the HUD numbers (start, 5 min, 10 min) and heat notes into this issue. The agent ships measurement and adaptivity first, then tunes from the owner's numbers in a follow-up if needed.
