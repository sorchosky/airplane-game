# #225 Boats: moored fishing boats and drifting sailboats

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/225

## Context
The town (#224) needs life on the water. Motion has to cost nothing on the CPU (the "no React state
at frame rate" rule and #60's zero-allocation rule). The water is now the 5 × 8 km inland sea
(#223), so sailboats have plenty of room.

## Goal
3 or 4 fishing boats at the dock and 2 or 3 sailboats out on the sea, gently alive.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Models.** Fishing boat (hull, small wheelhouse, mast with a boom) and sailboat (hull, mast,
      white mainsail and jib) from the landmark kit. Hull colours from the town palette tokens.
      ≤ 400 triangles each.
- [ ] **Motion in the vertex shader.** A per-vertex phase attribute drives bob (±0.25 m, 4 to 6 s
      period) and roll (±3°). Sails add a slow belly sway. Sailboats drift along closed loops on
      the sea at 1 to 2 m/s, within 3 km of the town so they read from the station, computed from
      the game clock in the shader or one uniform per boat. No per-frame JS beyond uniform writes.
- [ ] Moored boats sit at the finger piers from #224. Sailboats stay at least 60 m off any shore,
      islands included.
- [ ] One instanced or merged draw for all boats, plus the outline draw.
- [ ] Motion freezes under `prefers-reduced-motion` and at `?shot=` so captures are deterministic.

## Out of scope
Wakes and spray. Boats elsewhere in the world.
