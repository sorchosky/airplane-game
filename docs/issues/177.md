# #177 Wrapping world: wrap the plane and world-anchored systems

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/177

Part of #167.

## Context

Once terrain repeats with period P (#176), the plane can wrap position silently. That also keeps coordinates near the origin on long flights. Terrain streaming, foliage caches, landmarks, the golden path, clouds (which already use a wrap window), contact effects and replay all assume unbounded coordinates.

## Goal

Flying straight in any direction returns over the home basin with no visible jump, and every world-anchored system follows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] When the plane leaves [−P/2, P/2) on x or z, the sim position shifts by ±P in the same frame as the camera, vortex ribbons and any per-frame history. A pure `wrapPosition` with tests.
- [ ] Terrain chunks and foliage caches key on wrapped chunk coordinates, so crossing the seam reuses chunks instead of rebuilding a band.
- [ ] Landmarks, golden path rings and cumulus are drawn at their nearest image to the camera.
- [ ] Replay and `?shot=` stay deterministic across a wrap (test with a replay fixture crossing the seam).
- [ ] e2e: a scripted keyboard flight crosses the seam with no frame-time spike over 2× the median (`?debug`).
