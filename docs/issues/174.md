# #174 River along the route's low stretches

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/174

Part of #167.

## Context

Rivers today follow zero crossings of a low-frequency noise (`heightfield.ts`), unrelated to the route. A river along the valley is the oldest wayfinding device there is. There is one water plane at `waterLevel` = 32 m, so a river can only run where the valley floor sits just above it, the stretches the route design (#170) marks as low.

## Goal

A river channel runs along the valley floor through the low stretches and into a lake in or near the home basin.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pure modifier cuts a channel below `waterLevel` along the route centreline wherever the designed floor is within the low band, with banks that ease into the floor. Width and depth in `terrainConfig`.
- [ ] The channel ends cleanly (into a lake scoop or by tapering above water) where the floor rises. No water appears on slopes.
- [ ] Noise rivers inside the valley corridor are suppressed so two rivers don't cross the floor. Outside it they stay.
- [ ] Unit tests: channel bed below `waterLevel` along the low stretches, banks above it, no channel outside them.
- [ ] Bookmarks, `?map` and `?shot=lake-bank` before and after.

## Out of scope

Flowing water shading. A second water level.
