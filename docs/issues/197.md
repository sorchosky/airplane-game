# #197 Camera preview goes blank during wings practice, returns at flying

State: open · Labels: milestone:M2, agent-ready, size:S · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/197

## Context
Found while testing #19 on device (2026-10-01). After calibration, the corner camera preview goes blank for the whole wings practice (the tutorial hints from #74). It comes back as soon as the hints finish and the game enters `flying`.

Pose tracking is **not** interrupted. The owner's recording from that run (1284 frames over 64 s) has no frame gap over 300 ms and no empty landmark frames. So the `<video>` keeps playing and MediaPipe keeps reading it. Only the visible preview is lost.

## Likely cause (unverified)
`CameraPreview` (`src/ui/CameraPreview.tsx`) reparents the single shared `<video>` from `cameraService` into its container on mount, and on unmount moves it back to `document.body` as a 1×1px, `opacity: 0` element. Last cleanup wins.

`FrontDoorStage` keeps the old beat mounted while it plays its exit (`useBeatTransition`, "lagging the game state while the old beat plays its exit"). So on calibrate → wings:
1. `WingsPrompts` mounts and its `CameraPreview` takes the video.
2. `CalibrateScreen` (still shown during its exit) unmounts later, and its `CameraPreview` cleanup moves the video back off-screen.
3. Nothing re-takes it until `Hud` mounts a new `CameraPreview` at `flying`.

That matches "blank during tutorial, back after".

## Goal
The camera preview stays visible and live through calibrate → wings → flying with no blank stretch.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Confirm or rule out the cause above (e.g. log mount/cleanup order, or check `getVideo().parentElement` during `wings`). Note the real cause in the PR.
- [ ] Fix so the video always ends up in the most recently mounted, still-mounted preview. One option: have the cleanup only return the video to `body` if it is still inside *this* preview's container, and have previews claim it through a small ownership stack in `cameraService` so an earlier preview unmounting late can't steal it back.
- [ ] Unit or component test covering two previews where the first unmounts after the second mounts. The video stays in the second.
- [ ] Playwright check (fake camera or replay with `controlMode=camera`): during `wings`, the `camera-preview` contains the `<video>` and it is visible.
- [ ] Lock-in contract animation (#63) into the corner still plays.

## Out of scope
Changing the beat transition timing or the wings flow itself.
