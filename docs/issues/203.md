# #203 Phone retest while casting, then close #27 and the M4 epic

State: open · Labels: milestone:M4, size:S, needs-human · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/203

## Context
The first 10-minute cast trace on the owner's phone (#27, 2026-10-01) found three things. The governor reached rung 8/8 within about 15 s, and the HUD showed `dpr 1.50` there because R3F re-applied the Canvas `dpr` prop and undid the governor's pixel-ratio steps. The TV mirror froze for most of the session while the phone rendered 24 to 45 fps, and recovered briefly near minute 9 at 45 fps in a sparse scene. Pose inference ran 26 to 37 ms on the main thread at 20 Hz, which blocks a frame per detection.

Fixes shipped on `ticket/27-governor-dpr-fix`: the Canvas pixel ratio now follows the governor, `?cast` renders at 30 fps with a 30 fps governor budget, and the debug readouts use a smaller type token. Numbers from that trace are in #27.
