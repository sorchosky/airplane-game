# #199 Warm-caption false positives: run the diagnostic on your phone

State: open · Labels: needs-human · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/199

## Context

The "Your phone is getting warm" caption (#61) shows early and often, with no sign the device is actually hot. It never reads temperature, because browsers don't expose it. It fires when the frame-time p95 stays above 33 ms on the `low` quality tier for 10 s (`src/app/robustness.ts`, `stepThermalWatch`).

Likely causes, not yet confirmed on a device:

1. **A 30 fps phone counts as slow.** A steady 30 fps frame is 33.3 ms and the threshold is 33. Low Power Mode, an AirPlay or Chromecast mirroring cap, or a 30 Hz display mode all trip it with nothing struggling.
2. **Reaching `low` is easy on phones.** The governor budgets 60 fps (16.7 ms). Phones also run pose detection and mirroring, so they miss it, walk down the ladder in about 4 s per rung, and reach `low` around 20 s. The 10 s sustain timer then fires near 30 s into a flight.
3. **The warm watch samples more roughly than the governor.** `PerfProbe` uses a 5 s window with no settle time and no stall filter, so launch-time shader compiles and old slow frames feed it.

A diagnostic is in the linked PR. It adds a `warm watch N/10s` line to the `?debug` perf HUD. When the caption fires it shows `WARM fired at <s>: <ms>, <cause>?`, where cause is `refresh-cap` (steady ~30 fps) or `slow-frames` (uneven or slower). It also logs the last 2 minutes of samples to the console.

## What you need to do

1. Open the Vercel preview for the diagnostic PR on your phone, with `?debug` on the URL. HTTPS is required for the camera.
2. Fly the way you normally do, with the phone cast to the TV, until the caption appears. Keep the debug HUD readable on the TV.
3. When it fires, note the HUD line: `WARM fired at ...`.
4. Also note the `rung` and `dpr` lines and the `fps` line from the HUD at that moment. A photo or screenshot of the TV is fine.
5. Check whether Low Power Mode is on (iOS: Settings, Battery) and whether you were mirroring. Repeat once with Low Power Mode off and, if you can, once without mirroring.
6. Comment the results here, then remove `needs-human`.

## Decision

| Result | Next step | Who |
|---|---|---|
| `refresh-cap` | Stop treating a 30 fps cadence as slow: raise `p95ThresholdMs` to about 40 and judge on tier plus variance. File an agent ticket. | agent |
| `slow-frames`, tier already `low`, rung at bottom | Real performance problem. Profile pose inference and the scene on that device. File a perf ticket. | agent, with your device numbers |
| `slow-frames` only during the first ~20 s | Launch-time hitches feed the watch. Add a settle time and a stall filter to match the governor. File an agent ticket. | agent |
| Never fires with `?debug` | Note device, iOS version and browser here. The signal is device-specific. | you |
