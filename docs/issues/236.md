# #236 Course rings: show the next three, toggle the full course, point along the flow

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/236

## Context
Owner report, 2026-10-04: at the start of a flight it is easy to lose track of where the loop is
and which way it flows. The golden path (#175) already places 16 wind rings about 750 m apart
(`GATE_STATIONS` in `src/world/routePoints.ts`, drawn by `src/world/GoldenPath.tsx`), plus the cloud
gate and the return-notch lap line. Today every ring is always on, drawn as a thin pale hoop at
0.38 to 0.55 opacity, with nothing that says which way to fly through it. From a couch the rings
are hard to see and give no direction.

## Goal
By default the player sees only the next three rings ahead, clearly, each pointing along the
loop's direction of travel. A setting shows the whole course instead, from the control choice
screen and the pause menu.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Next three by default.** A pure `nextGates(s, count)` returns the next `count` ring gates
      ahead of route position `s`, wrapping past the notch into the next lap. `s` is the plane's
      nearest route point (`ROUTE.nearest`), not lap progress, so a player who wanders off and
      comes back sees the rings ahead of where they rejoin. If the plane is more than 1.5 km from
      the route, `s` comes from the nearest route point anyway, so the rings always point back
      into the loop.
- [ ] **Hierarchy.** Next ring at full strength, second at 70%, third at 45%. Rings entering the set
      scale and fade in over 400 ms, rings leaving fade out over 400 ms. Instant under reduced
      motion.
- [ ] **Full course.** With the setting on `all`, every ring shows at the current strengths, still
      with the next three emphasised as above.
- [ ] **Flow direction.** Each visible ring carries 3 chevrons inside its disc pointing along the
      route tangent at that gate, with a marching pulse along the tangent on a 1.2 s loop (vertex
      or fragment shader, no per-frame JS). Static under reduced motion and at `?shot=`. The ring
      plane faces the tangent so you always fly through it head-on.
- [ ] **Readable from the couch.** Thicker ring tube and a warm gold emissive token (new, e.g.
      `color.courseRing`) with toon stepping and an outline, so the next ring reads at 750 m on a
      1080p TV in every lighting preset and at night. Contrast ≥ 3:1 against sky and grass,
      asserted in `tokens.test.ts`.
- [ ] **Setting.** `courseRings: 'next3' | 'all'`, default `next3`, persisted with the same
      load/save pattern as `src/app/accessibilitySettings.ts` (its own storage key is fine), with
      a `?rings=all` URL flag.
- [ ] **Pause menu.** New item "Course rings: next 3" / "Course rings: all" after Captions, toggled
      by the same gesture, keyboard and touch paths as the other toggles. Strings in
      `src/ui/copy.ts`.
- [ ] **Control choice screen.** A secondary toggle under the two choices with the same label, at
      `tv-caption` size, a 48 px minimum target, focusable, styled as a quiet hairline control so
      it never competes with the two primary choices. Toggling does not leave the screen, so the
      control choice stays the last touch.
- [ ] Gameplay unchanged: speed gain, lap counting, the cloud gate and the notch work as today.
      Hidden rings still count if crossed.
- [ ] **Budget.** Draws +1 at most (chevrons). No per-frame allocations (#60).
- [ ] Unit tests: `nextGates` including wrap and off-route, the setting parser and URL flag. e2e:
      toggle in the pause menu by keyboard and on the control choice screen by click, and assert
      the visible ring count (3 vs all) through the test hook.
