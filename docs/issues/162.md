# #162 [Epic] Sky and time · Cloud gate, stars, local-time start and sun and moon dial

State: open · Labels: epic, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/162

## Goal

Fix the sky and time issues from the 2026-10-01 in-flight QA screenshot: the faceted cloud gate, stars showing on a daylight sky, the clock's filled pill, and the fixed 07:00 start. Coordination issue, not an implementation ticket.

## Context

Owner QA on 2026-10-01 (in-game 05:30, iPhone). The golden path's cloud gate is 11 unlit translucent icosahedrons that read as stacked hexagons and keep their mount-time colour. Stars follow `atmoNight` (the colour keys), not sky darkness, so they show on a blue dawn sky. The `HH:MM` pill is a filled slab against art bible §8.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-01)

- The title shows a fixed time that maps to the player's local time. When flight begins the clock advances from that same minute at one day per 5 real minutes. No 07:00 default and no jump.
- The saved-time resume (#94) is removed.
- No night clamp.
- The new sun and moon dial has no digits, and neither does the pause menu. The numeric time lives only in the `?debug` overlay and the dial's `aria-label`.
