# #223 Large lake on the east river reach

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/223

## Context
Owner direction, 2026-10-04: add a large lake with a small fishing town as a landmark. There is one
water plane at `waterLevel` 32 m (#167 constraints), so a lake is terrain carved below it. The
route's low stretches are the east river (s 5.4 to 6.6 km, floor 34 to 36 m) and the west river
(s 12.0 km, beside the arch). The east reach has the room, sits in the long 2.0 to 7.1 km gap
between the tower and the waterfall, and is outside the basin, so the title flyby and spawn are
untouched.

## Goal
A lake about 1.6 × 1.0 km opens east of the east river reach, fed by the river, revealed by a
break in the valley wall and framed at a new station.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Placement.** The PR's first commit posts a `?map` proposal: lake centre, outline, shore
      height and the town's shore site. Rules: lake edge 150 to 400 m east of the route, station
      at least 1.2 km from the waterfall station (move the waterfall station later along the route
      if needed and say so), river enters the lake's south or west shore.
- [ ] **Shape.** An irregular outline (not an ellipse), from a polar radius with 3 to 5 noise
      octaves. Flat bed 6 m under the water plane, shelving shallows 40 to 80 m wide, one
      peninsula or island. Shore rises to 36 to 42 m meadow, then hills.
- [ ] **Reveal and station.** A new `LANDMARK_STATIONS` entry `town` (`kind` added). The valley's
      east wall breaks open so the lake is hidden before reveal `s` and in the 60° chase view at the
      station, per the existing reveal tests extended to the new kind.
- [ ] Foliage exclusions cover the lake and a 120 m flat town pad on the chosen shore.
- [ ] Wrap seamless. Chunk build time within +5%.
