# #223 Inland sea east of the loop, with islands

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/223

## Context
Owner direction, 2026-10-04: add a large body of water with a small fishing town as a landmark,
ocean in scale, so it feels like a soft edge that persuades players to stay near the loop. It is
not a hard boundary. Flight stays free per the #167 decisions, and the world still wraps every
24 km. It works because there is about 2 minutes of open water with nothing to fly to, and the
haze (full sky colour at 9 km) dissolves the far shore so it reads as ocean from the town.

There is one water plane at `waterLevel` 32 m (#167 constraints), so the sea is terrain carved
below it. The east river reach (s 5.4 to 6.6 km, floor 34 to 36 m) sits in the long 2.0 to 7.1 km
gap between the tower and the waterfall, and is outside the basin, so the title flyby and spawn
are untouched.

## Goal
An inland sea about 5 × 8 km opens east of the east river reach, fed by the river, with 2 or 3
islands, revealed by a break in the valley wall and framed at a new station.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Placement.** The PR's first commit posts a `?map=24` proposal matching the sketch: outline,
      shore heights, island sites and the town's shore site. Rules: west shore 150 to 400 m east of
      the route, station at least 1.2 km from the waterfall station (move the waterfall station
      later along the route if needed and say so), river enters the west shore south of the town.
- [ ] **Coast.** An irregular outline from a polar radius with 4 to 6 noise octaves, with at least
      3 bays and 2 headlands. Bed 6 m under the water plane beyond the shallows, shelving shallows
      40 to 120 m wide, wider on the islands. Shore rises to 36 to 42 m beach and meadow, then hills.
      The east and far shores may rise straight into hills since they mostly sit in haze.
- [ ] **Islands.** 2 or 3 islands, 400 to 900 m across, low (peak 20 to 45 m above water) with wide
      beaches, at least 2 km from the west shore and 1 km from each other. One is at least 1.2 km
      across for the resort in #235. Return their centres and radii from a pure function so #235
      and foliage can use them.
- [ ] **Reveal and station.** A new `LANDMARK_STATIONS` entry `town` (`kind` added). The valley's
      east wall breaks open so the sea is hidden before reveal `s` and in the 60° chase view at the
      station, per the existing reveal tests extended to the new kind.
- [ ] Foliage exclusions cover the sea and a 120 m flat town pad on the west shore. Island foliage
      is left to #235.
- [ ] Wrap seamless. Chunk build time within +5% (water area should make it faster). Triangles at
      the new `lake-town` bookmark ≤ the same view on `main`.
