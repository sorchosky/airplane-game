# #157 Title flyby: scripted plane loop and title camera

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/157

Part of #151.

## Context

After #153 ("Live world behind the front door"), the plane is parked. The title needs it flying a composed, repeatable loop. A scripted path (not the sim on autopilot) keeps the left-third masthead clear and makes screenshots deterministic. Pure logic stays pure per `CLAUDE.md`.

## Goal

Fly the plane along a seamless scripted loop with a title camera that keeps it in the right two thirds, and expose a "lean in" framing for the Choose beat.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pure module (`src/flight/titleFlyby.ts`, plain TS, Three math types allowed) returns plane position, orientation and bank for time `t` on a closed 40 to 60 s loop near the spawn valley. Position and tangent continuous at the seam (unit test). Bank follows curvature, max 25°.
- [ ] The module returns the camera pose for `t` and a blend factor `lean` (0 = masthead framing, 1 = Choose framing, plane closer and centred behind the frames). A unit test samples the loop at 1 s steps: at `lean = 0` the plane's projected position stays within x ∈ [0.4, 0.92], y ∈ [0.45, 0.85] at 16:9 (normalized, origin top left).
- [ ] The loop stays at least 60 m above `surfaceHeightAt` (unit test against the heightfield).
- [ ] The plane uses its existing model, prop disc and control-surface articulation driven by the scripted bank. Wingtip vortices allowed, no contact effects.
- [ ] `?shot=title` freezes the loop at a named `t`. Reduced motion shows that frozen pose.
- [ ] No per-frame allocations (pattern in `src/flight/allocations.test.ts`).
