# #230 Title flyby framing: lower horizon, bigger sky

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/230

## Context
In the current title the bottom half is flat green with nothing in it, and the horizon sits about
33% down from the top, so a vertically centred masthead crosses the busy horizon line. BotW key art
is about two thirds sky. The flyby is scripted in `src/flight/titleFlyby.ts` and framed by
`src/flight/TitleCamera.tsx` (2026-10-01 #157 decision).

![Title before, 1920×1080](https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/blob/concept-art-2026-10-04/docs/concepts/2026-10-04-title-current-1920.png?raw=true)

Repo path for agents that can't render images: `docs/concepts/2026-10-04-title-current-1920.png`
on branch `concept-art-2026-10-04` (`git fetch origin concept-art-2026-10-04`).

## Goal
The title frame is mostly sky, the masthead sits over sky at every preset, and the plane stays in
the right two thirds.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] At the frozen t = 6 s (`?shot=title`, reduced motion) the horizon sits 58 to 62% down from
      the top of the frame at 1920×1080 and 844×390.
- [ ] Across the whole 48 s loop the horizon stays 52 to 66% down from the top, and the plane stays
      right of 33vw.
- [ ] The 48 s loop path, altitude and bank are unchanged. Only camera pitch and offset change.
- [ ] The Choose beat's blend to the closer centred framing still lands on the same end framing.
- [ ] The masthead contrast gate from #229 passes at every lighting preset.
- [ ] Unit test in `titleFlyby.test.ts` or a camera math test for the horizon band.

## Out of scope
Masthead styling (#229). New flyby paths.
