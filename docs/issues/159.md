# #159 Beat: Masthead to Choose

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/159

Part of #151.

## Context

With the stage, masthead and `ControlChoice` in place, Start still hard-cuts to the choice. The owner wants the title to give way to the controls over a blurred version of the live scene, as one continuous move. Blur is rendered in the pipeline, not with `backdrop-filter` (decision files from #152).

## Goal

Start transforms the masthead into the Choose beat: wordmark becomes a running head, the world leans in and softens, the frames draw in.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Timeline from the Start tap, constants in one exported object, scheduling helpers unit tested:
  - 0 to 300 ms: Start and the rule fade out.
  - 0 to 700 ms: the wordmark shrinks to `tv-caption` scale and glides to a top-left running head inset by the safe area (shared-element move), dropping to 70% opacity.
  - 0 to 900 ms: the title camera's `lean` eases 0 → 1 and the world blur ramps to full.
  - 400 to 800 ms: header fades in, rising 8 px.
  - 500 to 1100 ms: each frame's corner marks draw in from the corners toward their rest positions, staggered 80 ms. Glyphs fade in as their marks land and start looping.
  - Ease out throughout, no overshoot.
- [ ] Blur on `medium` and `high`: a downsampled blur pass in the composer (`src/render/`), one strength uniform, plus 20% desaturation and the cool scrim tint from art bible §8. Zero cost at strength 0.
- [ ] Blur on `low` (no composer): the world freezes to a still on Start and CSS-blurs that still once. No `backdrop-filter` over the live canvas on any tier.
- [ ] Back (Escape, browser back) reverses the timeline to the settled masthead.
- [ ] Reduced motion: 200 ms crossfade, no camera move, static blur, running head appears in place.
- [ ] `TitleHandoff` and its snapshot store removed if nothing else uses them.
- [ ] Frame time during the transition on `medium` recorded in the PR (`?debug`).
