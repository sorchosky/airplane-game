# #3 [Epic] M2 Gesture control

State: open · Labels: epic, milestone:M2 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/3

## Goal
Fly with your body. The front camera plus MediaPipe pose detection drives the same `ControlInput` the keyboard does. There's a mirrored camera preview in the top left with a thin orientation line, and control is only live while arms are outstretched.

## Done when
- All sub-issues are closed.
- Arms out engages control, tilting banks the plane, raising or lowering both arms pitches it.
- Arms down hands off to autopilot with a prompt. Arms down for 5 s pauses.
- A fake-camera Playwright test exercises the whole pipeline in CI.

## Owner checkpoint
Prop the phone in landscape, cast to the TV, and fly. Report latency, jitter and any false engage or disengage.
