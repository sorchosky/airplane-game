# #163 Cloud gate rebuilt from cumulus heaps

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/163

Part of #162.

## Context

The golden path's cloud gate (`src/world/GoldenPath.tsx`) draws 11 `IcosahedronGeometry(1, 0)` meshes with an unlit `MeshBasicMaterial` at 42% opacity. On screen they read as overlapping translucent hexagons (owner QA, 2026-10-01, in-game 05:30), unlike the toon-shaded cumulus heaps (#70, `src/world/Clouds.tsx`, `src/world/cloudMath.ts`) and the high stratus the owner likes. Its colour is captured once from `activeLighting()` at mount, so it goes stale as the day cycle moves.

## Goal

The cloud gate is a dense cluster of the same cumulus heaps used everywhere else, lit by the live day cycle.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The 11 icosahedron meshes and their material are removed.
- [ ] The gate is drawn by the existing cumulus instanced mesh: extra heap instances appended at the gate position (a pure layout function in `cloudMath.ts`, with a unit test on count, bounds and determinism). No new draw call and no new material.
- [ ] The gate reads as a cloud bank the plane flies into: at least 8 heaps, sized within the existing `puffRadiusMin`/`puffRadiusMax`, centred on `route.cloud`, overlapping enough that no gap wider than the plane's wingspan opens on the route line (unit test).
- [ ] Entering the gate still triggers the burst, veil and audio through `cloudStore` exactly as now. The existing gate trigger radius is unchanged.
- [ ] The gate's colour follows the day cycle (it uses the cumulus material's live uniforms).
- [ ] `?shot=clouds` and a new `?shot=cloud-gate` bookmark captured before and after at 1920×1080, at `?time=05:30` and `?time=12:00`.

## Out of scope

Cumulus shading changes. Where the gate sits on the route (#175 may move it).
