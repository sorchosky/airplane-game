# #221 Roughen the route valley so the loop reads as natural terrain

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/221

## Context
Owner review, 2026-10-04: the loop looks artificial. The `?map` view shows why. The valley from
#172 is a constant-profile trench swept along a smooth Catmull-Rom ellipse. Its width varies only
between the 12 route points, both walls rise over the same 180 m to the same 100 m `wallLift`
shoulder, and the shoulders draw as parallel contour rings round the whole loop
(`docs/screenshots/174-map-10km-after.png`). In flight the walls read as smooth beige berms
(`docs/screenshots/176-valley-tower-approach-after.png`). BotW valleys are asymmetric. One side is
a cliff, the other a grassy slope. The floor wanders, side gullies cut in, and the width breathes.

Route data (`ROUTE`, `LANDMARK_STATIONS`, golden-path gates, the river) stays as is. This ticket
changes only the carved terrain around the route.

![Current loop on ?map=10, showing the parallel shoulder rings](https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/blob/main/docs/screenshots/174-map-10km-after.png?raw=true)

## Goal
The valley follows the loop but no longer looks swept. From the air and on the map the corridor
reads as eroded terrain with varied width, asymmetric walls and side valleys.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Width breathes along `s`.** Valley half-width is modulated by low-frequency 1D noise along
      arc length (wavelength 600 to 1200 m, ±25% of the route's `valleyWidth`), clamped so it never
      goes under 150 m half-width or past the 300 to 600 m range in `docs/world-route.md`.
- [ ] **Asymmetric walls.** Left and right wall run (`wallShare` × flank) and shoulder lift vary
      independently along `s`. One side steepens to cliff (over 45°, so it picks up the rock band)
      while the other relaxes to a 15 to 25° grass slope, swapping sides every 1 to 3 km.
- [ ] **Floor meander.** The carved floor centre offsets from the route by ±40 m of low-frequency
      noise. The route line itself stays at least 100 m inside the floor everywhere, so the golden
      path, river and stations are untouched. The river follows the carved centre, not the route
      line, if that reads better. Note the choice in the PR.
- [ ] **Shoulders break up.** Shoulder heights get a ridged-noise term (±40 m) so the rim is a
      broken skyline, not a level contour. At least 4 side gullies (60 to 120 m wide, 300 to 600 m
      long) open off the valley at random `s`, avoiding the 300 m around each landmark station.
- [ ] **Reveals still hold.** Each landmark is still hidden before its reveal `s` and framed at
      its station per `docs/world-route.md`. The existing reveal and sightline tests pass.
- [ ] Surface stays C1 (smoothstep blends only). No new seams at the notches or at the wrap.
- [ ] Chunk build time within +10% of `main`, measured the same way #172 did.
- [ ] Pure functions in `routeValley.ts` with unit tests for the clamps (min width, route stays
      inside the floor, station clearances).

## Out of scope
New mountains outside the valley shoulders and the basin rim (#222). Rock meshes (#231). Changing
the route spline. See handoff note below.
