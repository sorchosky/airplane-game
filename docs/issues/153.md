# #153 Live world behind the front door

State: open · Labels: size:M, agent-ready, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/153

Part of #151.

## Context

`FlightScene` mounts only in `wings`, `flying` and `paused` (`src/app/App.tsx`). The title draws a separate WebGL2 shader (`src/ui/TitleSky.tsx`), and Start covers the R3F scene's boot with a JPEG snapshot (`TitleHandoff`). The continuous front door needs the real world from the first second, on one canvas that never remounts.

## Goal

Mount the world from `title` onward on one persistent canvas, booting behind a `TitleSky` poster, at a capped frame rate, with the plane parked and a fixed establishing camera.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `FlightScene` (or a thin wrapper) takes a scene mode, `attract` or `flight`. In `attract` the flight sim doesn't step, input sources aren't attached, and no HUD, prompts or touch controls render.
- [ ] The world renders at the clock's held minute (the player's local time on a fresh page load, once #165 ("Day cycle starts at the player's local time") lands), frozen until flight begins.
- [ ] The world mounts in `title` and stays mounted on the same canvas and WebGL context through `select`, `permission`, `calibrate`, `wings`, `flying` and `paused`. Quit to title returns it to `attract` without remounting.
- [ ] While the first terrain chunks under the camera aren't ready, `TitleSky` shows as a poster. When ready, the world fades in over 600 ms and `TitleSky` unmounts, leaving one WebGL context. The readiness check is a pure function with unit tests.
- [ ] If the world fails to start (context loss or WebGL error at boot), `TitleSky` stays and the title remains usable.
- [ ] `attract` renders at most 30 fps via a frame limiter and stops rendering while the document is hidden.
- [ ] New `?shot=title` bookmark in `src/debug/shots.ts`: plane parked in the right two thirds, horizon in the upper third, open sky in the left third.
- [ ] Reduced motion renders one frame and holds it.
- [ ] The camera and pose services still start only after the control choice. No model download on page load.
- [ ] Title frame time on the `low` tier recorded in the PR before and after (`?debug`).

## Out of scope

Plane motion (Title flyby). The overlay stage, masthead and beats.
