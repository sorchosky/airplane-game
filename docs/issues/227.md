# #227 Quality ladder keeps resolution: pixel ratio goes last, never below 1.0

State: open · Labels: milestone:M4, agent-ready, size:S · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/227

## Context
Owner report: resolution dips really low at times. `buildLadder` in
`src/render/adaptiveQuality.ts` gives up pixel ratio first (1.5, 1.25, 1, 0.75) before post tier,
the 30 fps cap, foliage and view distance. Any 3 s over budget drops a rung, and climbing back
needs 10 s under 70% of budget per rung. So a terrain-chunk swap or a landmark shader compile can
cost a whole resolution step, and the bottom rung renders an 844×390 phone at 633×293, upscaled to
a 1080p TV. In a cel-shaded look that mostly breaks the outlines and the flat colour edges, which
are the style. Post FX (bloom, god rays, grade) costs more and shows less on a phone.

## Goal
When the phone struggles, it gives up effects and distance before resolution, and resolution never
drops below a pixel ratio of 1.0.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] New ladder order: post tier `high` → `medium`, foliage 0.6, dpr 1.5 → 1.25, 30 fps cap, view
      distance 7 km, foliage 0.3, post tier `low`, dpr 1.0. `DPR_STEPS` loses 0.75.
- [ ] `describeRung` and the perf HUD rung line reflect the new order.
- [ ] Over-budget timing ignores frames inside a 500 ms window after a terrain tile swap or a
      first-use shader compile, if those events are already observable. If not, note it in
      handoff instead of adding plumbing.
- [ ] Landmark, cloud and water materials are compiled during the title (`renderer.compileAsync`
      or a warm-up frame) so their first reveal doesn't trigger a step. Skip if already done, and
      say so.
- [ ] `adaptiveQuality.test.ts` covers the new order, the 1.0 floor and the hitch window.
- [ ] Add this ticket to #203's "Wait for" list (edit the issue) so the phone retest covers it.

## Out of scope
Dynamic resolution scaling and temporal upscaling. Warm caption logic (#228).
