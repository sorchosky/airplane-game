## Design direction and boundaries

- **Plane**. Neutral charcoal/near-black at dawn/day, restrained neutral lift only where needed at night. Fix both reported times together and verify the whole interpolation.
- **Foliage**. Diagnose rim lighting separately from actual hull gaps. Reduce the white sticker effect, preserve broad toon shading, and use lighter visual emphasis than the plane.
- **Mountains**. Fix periodic shader striping before considering assets. A JPG alone does not determine mapping, repetition, softness, or integration with light. The procedural-first approach honors the existing no-ground-textures decision.
- **Ground**. Remove the separate sparse grass cards and use the existing subtle terrain variation. Compare a flat baseline in the PR without shipping a new setting.
- **VFX**. Remove the specifically rejected birds and vertical wind streaks. Retain wingtip trails and useful contact/route effects.
- **HUD**. Default teaching becomes panel-free with a shadow and fade. Preserve the explicit high-contrast option. The flying action says “Pause”, the paused screen heading remains “Paused”.

The bird/streak, grass-card, and rock-strata changes intentionally revise prior art requirements at the owner's request. Child PRs must record narrowly scoped superseding decisions in new files, not rewrite decision history.
