# #170 Route design proposal: home basin, the cut, stations

State: open · Labels: agent-ready, size:S, review:visual, milestone:M5 · https://github.com/sorchosky/airplane-game/issues/170

Part of #167.

## Context

With `?map` and the route module in place, the loop needs designing on the real terrain. It's a design ticket: the agent proposes, the owner approves on the PR before any terrain change. The PR must not merge without the owner's approval.

## Goal

A route design document and control-point data the owner approves.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/world-route.md` with: the home basin (radius, ridge height, spawn position and heading), the cut (notch position facing spawn's heading, width at least 250 m), the return notch, the loop's control points, and one station per landmark (tower, arch, waterfall, tree, ruins) with its arc length and the bend that reveals it.
- [ ] Loop length 12.5 to 14.5 km (about one in-game day at 45 m/s cruise). Valley floor width 300 to 600 m. Floor heights noted, with the low stretches (floor near `waterLevel`) marked for the river.
- [ ] Each landmark is first visible from the route at least 1.5 km before its station, and a bend frames it within the chase camera's view (document the sightline per station).
- [ ] The home basin leaves room for the title flyby loop (#157) so the title shows the basin and the cut.
- [ ] `src/world/routePoints.ts` holds the real control points.
- [ ] `?map` images attached: current terrain with the route overlaid, and a sketch of where the basin and valley will change it.
- [ ] Owner approval recorded as a decision file: `docs/decisions/<date>-<issue#>-the-loop.md`.

## Out of scope

Terrain code.
